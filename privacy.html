Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026. This project privacy notice describes how Career Sarthii handles data in its local runnable version. The site owner's legal name and contact details must be added before public launch; have the policy reviewed for your deployment.

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, gender, email address, a salted password hash, category and qualification.
  • Preference data: selected exam. Language and theme are kept in browser storage.
  • Activity data: practice results, question timing, interview answers and summaries, and chat messages.
  • Study documents you upload, including their file contents.
  • Basic server logs may include network and request details, depending on how you run the server.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and show the exams you are eligible for.
  • To provide practice, explanations, model papers, interview practice and performance analysis.
  • To send exam notification, form and result alerts you have asked for.
  • To improve the service and keep it secure.

3. AI processing

If the site owner configures a Gemini API key, chat messages and interview answers are sent to Google's Gemini API to produce replies or coaching feedback. During AI chat, up to three recent PDF or TXT study documents may also be sent as context. No study files are sent when the Gemini key is not configured. AI output can be wrong, so verify important information.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. In this local version, account data and uploaded files are stored in the MongoDB database selected by the site owner. If Gemini is configured, the content described above is shared with Google for AI processing. A public deployment may involve additional hosting providers.

5. Storage and security

Account data and uploaded files are stored in the MongoDB database configured by the site owner. Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes, and sessions use an HttpOnly cookie. Use a restricted database user, protect the connection URI, and use HTTPS before public deployment.

6. Retention

Data remains in the database while the account exists. The dashboard's delete-account control removes the account, uploaded files, and associated activity from the local database.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or close your account, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law. Write to us at the address below.

8. Cookies and local storage

An HttpOnly cookie keeps you signed in for up to 14 days. Browser local storage remembers language and theme preferences. This project does not include analytics or advertising cookies.

9. Children

Career Sarthii is meant for users aged 18 and above, or minors using it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
